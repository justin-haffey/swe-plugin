# Dialectical Analysis

## Core question

Make architectural contradictions explicit and find a synthesis more capable than choosing one pole by default.

## Analyze

- Identify tensions evidenced in goals, architecture, contracts, ownership, implementation, incidents, or repeated decisions.
- State each pole charitably, including the outcome it protects and the costs it imposes.
- Determine whether the tension is a true contradiction, a scope mismatch, a temporal sequence, or a constraint that can be separated.
- Explore syntheses such as federated governance, stable core with variable edge, policy versus mechanism separation, or staged consistency where the evidence supports them.
- Trace the synthesis through authority, contracts, operations, failure modes, and migration.

## Recommendation test

A strong recommendation preserves the legitimate concern of both poles, changes the framing rather than averaging positions, and names where the residual tension remains.

## Avoid

Do not manufacture symmetry, compromise mechanically, or hide an unavoidable trade-off behind abstract language.
