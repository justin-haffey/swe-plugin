# First-Principles Reconstruction

## Core question

Separate fundamental truths from inherited choices and build a coherent alternative from the smallest defensible set of premises.

## Analyze

- Extract the target outcomes, non-negotiable invariants, external obligations, and measurable qualities from authoritative evidence.
- Classify current architecture choices as fundamental, contingent, historical, convenient, or unsupported.
- Temporarily discard contingent choices and derive the minimum responsibilities, state, flows, and contracts required by the fundamentals.
- Compare the reconstructed model with the current architecture to identify unnecessary structure and genuinely missing capability.
- Reintroduce unavoidable operational, compatibility, organizational, and migration constraints explicitly.

## Recommendation test

A strong recommendation derives from named premises, preserves verified constraints, and explains why its reconstruction is both simpler and sufficient. It identifies the riskiest premise to validate first.

## Avoid

Do not call preferences first principles, ignore compatibility or safety facts, or present a greenfield design as automatically superior.
