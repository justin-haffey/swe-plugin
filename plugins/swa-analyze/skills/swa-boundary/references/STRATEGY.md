# Boundary Critique

## Core question

Expose how the current framing includes, excludes, or marginalizes responsibilities and then propose more coherent boundaries.

## Analyze

- Identify the declared target boundary, its upstream and downstream authorities, and the responsibilities actually implemented inside it.
- Find work, data, decisions, actors, qualities, or failure handling that are excluded from the model but still shape outcomes.
- Find responsibilities inside the boundary that belong elsewhere, including duplicated ownership and implicit cross-boundary contracts.
- Compare alternative wider, narrower, or differently partitioned boundaries against cohesion, coupling, autonomy, operability, and migration cost.
- State which existing authority would have to accept each boundary change.

## Recommendation test

A strong recommendation makes ownership and contracts clearer, reduces harmful coupling or orphaned responsibility, and preserves legitimate authority boundaries. It explains what moves, what stays, and what new handoff is required.

## Avoid

Do not equate folders or repositories with correct conceptual boundaries, and do not recommend cross-repository writes as part of the analysis.
