# Concept

Apply [the delivery contract](../../references/ARTIFACT-CONTRACT.md).

Resolve the governing local Feature, optional Epic or explicit brief and actual research when relevant. Ordinary work may record the chosen approach in its Feature. Use references/CONCEPT-TEMPLATE.md beside owning work for distinct alternatives and a separate decision. Record outcome, alternatives, constraints, selected direction and consequences. Named human intent approval is default; authorized independent review retains Major human decisions and two-cycle limit. Do not create an Epic/research report solely as entry gates.
